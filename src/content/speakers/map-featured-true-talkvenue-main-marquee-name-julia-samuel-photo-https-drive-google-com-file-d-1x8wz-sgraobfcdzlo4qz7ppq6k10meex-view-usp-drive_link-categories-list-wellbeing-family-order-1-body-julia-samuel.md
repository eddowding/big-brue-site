Dame Prue Leith, now 86, is a restaurateur, writer and broadcaster. She made her name with a Michelin-starred restaurant and founded Leith’s School of Food and Wine, which she sold in 1995. She has written numerous cookery books, seven novels and her memoir *Relish*, and has been a columnist for the Daily Mail, The Guardian and the Daily Mirror.

A familiar television presence since the 1970s, she was a long-standing judge on *The Great British Menu* and later joined *The Great British Bake Off*. Beyond food, she has held roles across education and public life, including as Chancellor of Queen Margaret University. Her recent book is *Being Old...and learning to love it!*
